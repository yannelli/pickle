This build adds three Brine Royale events and three ways to bond with your pickle.

In the arena:
- Jar drops: every few minutes a jar appears on the minimap with a 10-second warning, then tips over and spills bonus food.
- Leader bounty: the biggest pickle wears a crown. Eating any of its pieces pays 10% extra mass.
- Run goals: each spawn gets three hidden goals. A short note pops up when you finish one, and each pays 5 coins within the daily coin cap. Dying or leaving shows a recap of peak mass, best rank, crunches, time alive, and goals, and you can share it.

At home:
- Wishes: up to twice a day (from 9am and from 3pm), your pickle asks for a food, a bath, a pet, a game, or a nap. Granting it within 6 hours gives +10 happy and 5 coins.
- Scrapbook: life events, a 32-slot elder album, and care-streak stickers at 3, 7, 14, and 30 days.
- Favorites: each variety has a favorite food and game worth +4 extra happy.

Please check:
1. Join Brine Royale and wait for a jar warning. Check the minimap marker, the jar tipping, and the food ring.
2. Find the crowned leader on the board and in the garden.
3. Finish a goal and check the pop-up, then leave. Check the recap card and that coins arrive in the closet.
4. After 9am, open the Nest and check for a wish. Grant it and check the reward.
5. Open the scrapbook and check the dates, the elder album, and your streak.
6. Feed your pickle its favorite food and check for the special reaction.
