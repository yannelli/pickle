This build updates Brine Royale movement and sizing. Smaller pickles move faster, and very large pickles keep growing visibly instead of looking the same size past one million mass.

Join Brine Royale and drag the joystick a short distance. Check that small drags start moving, that full speed arrives before the knob reaches its edge, and that releasing the stick stops your pickle.

Grow or split a large pickle and check that the camera keeps every piece in view and that danger outlines match what can eat you.

Report the device, iOS version, steps taken, expected result, actual result, and a screenshot or recording when useful.
