This build adds Game Center leaderboards and makes the app easier to get around.

What changed:
- Game Center: sign in to post your best scores to eight leaderboards: Brine Royale mass, the daily crunch, and each arcade game. Open them from the Play tab.
- Play tab: the arcade, Brine Royale, the daily crunch, and Pass & play are all in one place.
- Nest: the care buttons sit higher on the screen, and the Scrapbook has its own button.
- Settings: clearer sections, links to the privacy policy, privacy choices, terms, and support, and a separate page that explains how the app works.
- Closet: outfits are grouped by price.
- Clearer, plainer wording throughout.
