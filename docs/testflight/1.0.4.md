This build doubles the wardrobe to 36 outfits. Twelve new everyday looks cost 150 to 450 coins, and six premium looks are long-term goals: Brine raider (500), Pickle princess (1,000), Court jester (1,500), Dillicorn (2,000), Hot brine (2,500), and Dill emperor (3,000). Your pickle wears them at home and in Brine Royale, and web players see the same hats.

It also fixes a few arena details:
- Share cards keep large scores on one line, and the share message formats the number.
- Your personal best saves while you play, so closing the app mid-run keeps it.
- The Swap button is gone. Choose the joystick side in Settings under Brine Royale.
- The "Split to chase" tip is gone. The regroup countdown still appears while you are split.

Please check:
1. Open the wardrobe and scroll to the end. Check that every outfit draws on your pickle and that prices read correctly.
2. Unlock and wear a new outfit, join Brine Royale, and check that it appears on your pickle in the arena.
3. Grow a big run, then share it from the respawn card. Check that the mass fits on one line.
4. Turn on Settings > Brine Royale > Joystick on the right, join the arena, and check the controls swap sides.

Report the device, iOS version, steps taken, expected result, actual result, and a screenshot or recording when useful.
