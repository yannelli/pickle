A tiny care manual ↗

Use Feed for a sip of brine, Play to follow the heart as the jars shuffle, and Clean to tidy up. The 1, 2, 3 keys work, too. Tap your pickle to sleep or wake; sleep restores energy. A round costs 9 energy. Find the heart for +22 happiness, or get +8 for trying.

Your seed becomes a sprout at 30 seconds, a pickle at 5 minutes, and an elder at 30 minutes. Keep all four meters topped up. A very low meter can cause sickness; restore every meter to at least 30 to recover. Leaving a meter empty for 3 active minutes ends this little life, but you can plant a new one.

Progress saves automatically in this browser. While you're away, needs decay for at most 15 minutes, and your pickle can get sick but won't die. A sleeping pickle keeps resting. Care buttons make soft little beeps.
