Your Privacy Choices

This page explains the privacy choices you have in little dill and how to ask us to access, correct, or delete information. For what we collect and why, see the Privacy Policy.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral or targeted advertising. There are no ads in little dill. You do not need to opt out, and we honor Global Privacy Control signals automatically.

Choices in the Games

Care reminders (web)

Reminders are off until you turn them on. Choose Turn reminders off in the browser game to unsubscribe this device and delete its reminder record from our server. You can also block notifications in your browser settings.

Arena nickname

Other players in Brine Royale see your nickname. On the web you type it before each match. On iPhone and iPad it is your pickle's name, which you choose when it hatches. Use a name that is not your real name. Leaving a match with the exit button ends your reconnect window and removes the saved recovery copy.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

Game Center is optional. To stop sharing scores and achievements, or to change who can see your profile, sign out or adjust your settings in Settings > Game Center on your device.

Your pickle and saves

Your progress stays on your device. On the web, resetting the game or clearing this site's data in your browser removes it. On iPhone and iPad, choose Restart in the app or delete the app. .dill backups are files you keep and can delete at any time.

Requests to Know, Correct, or Delete

Email ryanyannelli@gmail.com with your request. Because there are no accounts, tell us what to look for: for example, the arena nickname and approximate time you played, or the browser and date you turned on reminders. Most game data is kept only for minutes or stays on your device, so we may have nothing to return.

We will confirm we received your request, verify it in a way that fits the data involved, and aim to respond within 45 days. You may use an authorized agent; we will need to verify both of you. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Appeals

If we decline a request, we will explain why. You may appeal by replying to that email or writing to ryanyannelli@gmail.com with "Privacy appeal" in the subject. We will answer the appeal in writing within 45 days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact your state attorney general.

Children's Information

A parent or guardian can ask us to delete information about their child by emailing ryanyannelli@gmail.com. We will delete what we hold and ask our providers to do the same.
